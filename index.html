Dynamic Ambulance Dispatch and Relocation

An event-driven Emergency Medical Services simulation for studying real-time ambulance dispatch and dynamic relocation under uncertain demand. The current framework evaluates MOVE-versus-STAY decisions using spatial demand, local coverage, relocation trajectories, and interactions among available ambulances.
